Evening Flock

Eighty birds. No conductor. Just three local rules.

Short trails show each bird’s recent turns.

Pause the sky, then take one beat at a time.

Turn on Hawk, then hold a finger in the sky. Let go and the flock regroups.

What makes a flock?

Coast removes neighbor steering. Keep apart keeps only the close-range push. Flock restores all three rules.

Turn away from birds that are too close.

Borrow the heading of nearby birds.

Steer toward nearby birds.

These are toy steering rules, not a measurement of a real bird flock.

Keep apart. Match direction. Stay near your neighbors. Together, small decisions become a flock.
