How to change one bird

Change this bird’s flight

Pause, turn on Neighbors and choose a bird. Turn this bird left changes only its heading. One beat lets the neighbors respond; Step back undoes the edit.

Turn this bird right rotates only the selected heading a quarter turn. The other birds keep their headings until a beat.

Turn this bird around reverses only the selected heading. Step back restores it before you try a different turn.

Rest this bird sets only the selected velocity to zero. Neighbor steering can start it again on the next beat.

Slow this bird halves only its velocity. Its heading stays the same; One beat lets the neighbors respond.

Speed up this bird doubles its velocity up to three world units per beat. Only this bird changes, and Step back restores it.

Borrow nearest flight copies the closest bird’s velocity inside the neighbor ring. It changes only this bird, not the flock rule.

Face nearest bird aims this bird at its closest neighbor inside the ring. Its speed stays the same; positions do not move until One beat.

Face away from nearest aims directly away from the closest neighbor inside the ring. Its speed stays the same, and Step back restores the sky.

Match nearest speed copies the closest neighbor’s speed, up to three world units per beat, while keeping this bird’s heading. A resting bird has no heading to keep.

Meet nearest flight halfway averages this bird’s velocity with its nearest neighbor’s. Only this bird changes; positions stay put. Opposite flights can cancel into rest. Step back restores the flight.

Counter nearest flight gives this bird the negative of its nearest neighbor’s velocity. The neighbor keeps flying as before. A resting neighbor gives this bird rest too. Step back restores the flight.

Change the neighboring flights

Trade flights with nearest swaps these two birds’ velocities, not their positions. The selected bird stays selected; Step back restores both flights.

Give the pair one flight gives both birds their average velocity. Their positions stay put, and equal opposite flights become rest. This is an edit, not a new flock rule. Step back restores both flights.

Rest this pair sets this bird and its nearest neighbor to zero velocity without moving either. One beat can let the flock rules steer them again. Step back restores both flights.

Turn this pair left rotates both velocities a quarter turn left. Neither bird moves until a beat. Step back restores both flights.

Turn this pair right rotates both velocities a quarter turn right. Neither bird moves until a beat. Step back restores both flights.

Turn this pair around reverses both velocities. Their speeds and positions stay the same. Step back restores both flights.

Slow this pair by half halves both velocities. It leaves the rest of the sky at its current pace. One beat shows the local difference; Step back restores both flights.

Rest only the nearest sets the nearest neighbor’s velocity to zero. This bird keeps its flight, and neither position changes. Step back restores the neighbor’s flight.

Give nearest this flight copies this bird’s velocity to its nearest neighbor. This bird keeps its own flight. The edit does not add a new flock rule; Step back restores the neighbor’s flight.

Move farther from nearest shifts this bird twelve world units away from its closest neighbor. Positions wrap across the sky edge; headings stay put.

Trade places with nearest swaps these two birds’ positions, not their headings. The selected bird stays selected; Step back restores both places.

Circle nearest left moves this bird a quarter turn around its closest neighbor across the wrapped sky. The gap stays the same; headings do not turn. Step back restores the position.

Circle nearest right moves this bird a quarter turn around its closest neighbor across the wrapped sky. The neighbor stays put; headings do not turn. Step back restores the position.

Bring both places closer halves the gap, moving both birds equally toward their shared midpoint. It does not slow either flight. One beat lets the closer neighbors react; Step back restores the places.

Try the flock rules

What each recipe keeps

Coast removes neighbor steering. Keep apart keeps only the close-range push. Match direction borrows nearby headings. Gather steers toward nearby birds. Flock combines all three rules.

Neighbor reach

A wider ring lets each bird listen farther away. The close-range push stays the same.

Starting sky

A passing gust

A gust nudges every heading once. The flock rules take it from there.

Flight pace

Slower flight gives you time to follow a turn. One beat stays one decision.
